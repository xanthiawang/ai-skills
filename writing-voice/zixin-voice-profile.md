## English academic voice — ten signature moves

1. **Open on a field-level tension, stated in one clause.** "The accumulating evidence on AI in education presents contradiction rather than consensus." One sentence names the fight; citations follow, never lead.
2. **"X rather than Y" is the signature contrast.** "contradiction rather than consensus", "quantity indicators rather than quality indicators", "risk mitigation rather than enhancement". This positive-form contrast is allowed and characteristic; it is distinct from the banned "not X, but Y" negative parallelism.
3. **Named-critique related work.** Every cited study gets: author, what they actually did, and one precise limitation. "Jin proposed RelianceScope ... but validated it only through self-report scales." Never a citation dump; never a limitation-free summary.
4. **Coin the concept, then use it as a plain noun.** First use: emphasized and defined ("a *cognitive offloading trap*, an asymmetrically self-reinforcing dynamic"). After that it is ordinary vocabulary. One coinage per paper, at most two.
5. **Numbers carry the argument.** Exact values inline in parentheses at the point of claim: (holdout κ = 0.734), (17.8% vs 3.4%, a 5.2-times asymmetry), (OR 1.91 [1.09, 3.33], p = .023). No claim of size without its number; no number without its comparison.
6. **Definitions arrive as citation + everyday example + boundary condition.** "Cognitive offloading refers to ... In everyday life ... calculators ... However, in educational contexts, the boundary ... becomes critical."
7. **Honest hedging placed AT the claim, never pooled at the end.** "which no longer clears the conventional threshold once exposure is counted correctly"; conclusions state what "awaits" further evidence. Every exploratory result labeled exploratory in the same sentence.
8. **Mechanism verbs, plain copulas.** characterize, redistribute, detect, predict, transfer, deliver; "is/has" over "serves as/features". Sentences average short-to-medium; one idea per sentence in Results.
9. **Structured abstracts for education journals** (Background/Purpose/Methods/Results/Conclusions); one dense narrative paragraph for CHI/HCI. Both end on what the study enables or requires next, stated concretely, never "future work will explore".
10. **Section titles are claims, sentence case.** "The Socratic content did not change behavior, and the learning pattern points to risk mitigation" — a reader gets the finding from the table of contents alone.

## Chinese academic/working voice

- 「」引用术语；表格优先于长段落；关键判断加粗；决策留痕（版本号+日期+改动原因一行）。
- 诚实降温是常规动作：每个正向结果后面紧跟"泼冷水"条目（先验混淆、自选择、多重比较），与英文 move 7 同构。
- 工作文档可用 ⭐/⚠️/✅ 语义标记；正式中文稿件不用 emoji、不用网络语，标点全角。
- 中英对照稿：技术术语保留英文（ICC、OR、glmer），数字与英文版逐位一致。
